# Methodology v3.2

1. The audited v3.1 spatial reconstruction is retained.
2. Nominal temporal histories are conditioned on the published 2022 map only by year; exact source date remains unknown.
3. Stable, uniform, decaying, accelerating, reactivated and step-change families are generated with time-dependent stages.
4. Full and focused survey campaigns are explicit; missingness has typed causes.
5. Leveling is adjusted from observed differences and independent datum constraints; synthetic truth is evaluation-only.
6. GNSS is stratified by profile and deformation intensity.
7. InSAR is generated as an independent continuous spatial-temporal field, not copied from survey-point trajectories.
8. Early acceleration is a separate rare-event classification task.
9. Model-ready features are physically separated from targets and latent parameters.
10. Real cycles must be evaluated with the frozen external harness and no retraining.
