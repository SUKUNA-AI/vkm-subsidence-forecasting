## Gate B — Classical, probabilistic and small-data evidence

B0–B4 cover frozen baselines and state-space/regime models. B5 freezes the
train-only evidence protocol; B6 performs nested temporal/profile/zone
screening of classical, probabilistic, boosting, glassbox, small neural and
neuro-fuzzy comparators. ETS, ARIMA/ARIMAX and
VAR are represented by formal data-geometry eligibility cards.

B5/B6 use only `t1_v1/train` and may produce only
`train_only_internal_research`; suite v4 is frozen before new holdout labels.
The completed outcome is `PASS_NO_NEW_PRIMARY`: B7 remains primary and no new
candidate passed all frozen gates.

## Gate C — Deep temporal models

LSTM, GRU, TCN, DeepAR, N-BEATS/N-HiTS, TFT, PatchTST, iTransformer. Five seeds, early stopping on validation, compute report.

The small B6 MLP and ENFS replica do not consume Gate C. Full sequence models
require a separate frozen Gate C protocol. The historical TabPFN row is
governance-excluded before scoring by `B6-GOV-001`.

## Gate D — Spatio-temporal models

STGCN, DCRNN, Graph WaveNet, GAT+TCN/GRU, ST Transformer. Graph must be built from geometry and mining context without future data.

## Gate E — Temporal foundation models and hybrid models

Zero/few-shot time-series foundation models, IMM+CatBoost, KalmanNet, Neural
ODE/CDE, physics-guided residuals and ensembles. B6 contains no foundation
model and is not a substitute for this temporal foundation-model gate.

## Gate F — Final validation

Temporal, rolling-origin, leave-profile, leave-zone, leave-block, transition and Monte Carlo OOD.

## Gate G — LLM/RAG support layer

Source-grounded explanation and reporting only. No numeric-primary or safety authority.

## Gate H — Thesis artefacts

Tables, figures, error atlas, model cards, reproducibility appendix and written sections.
